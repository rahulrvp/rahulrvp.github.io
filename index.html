About Me

I'm a Technical Lead with years of experience in mobile development, currently building and maintaining mobile SDKs at Mixpanel across Android, iOS, React Native, and Flutter platforms, designed to support large-scale customer deployments.

Previously, I led Android SDK engineering at Blueshift where I focused on designing, developing, and maintaining our Android SDK and other cross-platform plugins. Before that, at Fortelogic and Bullfinch, I led engineering teams across EduTech, MedTech, E-commerce, and TravelTech domains.

With an MCA from NIT Calicut, I bring a strong academic foundation to my technical expertise in building robust mobile solutions. Throughout my career, I've maintained hands-on development expertise in Android (Java/Kotlin), Flutter, and multiple cross-platform technologies. I'm passionate about creating high-quality mobile experiences that solve real business challenges while maintaining optimal performance and scalability.
